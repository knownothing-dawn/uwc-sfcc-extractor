# Data and copyright policy

The MIT License in this repository applies to the original source code,
schemas, synthetic tests and project documentation authored for this project.
It does not grant rights to third-party research papers or to content extracted
from them.

The public repository therefore excludes:

- journal and conference PDFs;
- publisher text, figure images and long quotations;
- manually digitized reference workbooks;
- per-paper `.internal` extraction packages;
- recorded-paper regression fixtures whose redistribution rights are unclear;
- local paths, author email addresses and other source metadata copied from PDFs.

Users are responsible for obtaining lawful access to source publications and for
complying with copyright, database rights, contractual restrictions and required
attribution when creating or sharing extracted datasets. Output generated by this
software is not automatically licensed under MIT.

When reporting a defect, prefer a minimal synthetic reproduction. A third-party
paper or extracted dataset may be linked only when it is already lawfully public
and its license clearly permits redistribution in the proposed form.

This policy is project guidance, not legal advice.
