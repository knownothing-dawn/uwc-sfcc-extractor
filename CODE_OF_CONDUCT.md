# Code of conduct

We want project discussions to be technically rigorous, welcoming and safe.

Participants must communicate respectfully, critique ideas rather than people,
avoid harassment and discrimination, and protect confidential or copyrighted
research material. Publishing another person's private data, credentials or
restricted paper content is unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that violate this policy and
may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report sensitive conduct
issues privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile; do not
post private evidence in a public issue.
